Department Assignment Rules

Department directors oversee their own department and may recommend or recruit coordinators, staff, and volunteers within their approved responsibilities. Appointments and portal access still require authorized review and activation.

Executive Leadership, Administration, and Board Governance are not departments and do not require department directors. Founder and Co-Founder designations remain outside the general position directory.
