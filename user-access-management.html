Department Assignment Rules

Department Directors lead their assigned department, recruit and recommend Coordinators, staff, and volunteers, and supervise work within their approved scope. Coordinators manage day-to-day schedules, communication, documentation, and team support. Department staff and volunteers work within assigned duties; Ambassadors are unpaid and belong to Events & Development.

Approval boundary: Recruitment or a proposed assignment never grants portal access. Executive approval and backend-enforced department permissions are required before activation. Directors cannot assign themselves executive roles, view unrelated departments, or bypass an approval.

Oversight: CEO and COO retain organization-wide executive oversight, subject to the existing separation-of-duties rules. Board roles remain separate from department assignments. Any changes to assignments must be audited by the secure backend.

Executive Leadership, Administration, and Board Governance are not departments and do not require department directors. Founder and Co-Founder designations remain outside the general position directory.
