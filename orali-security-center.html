Settings & Notifications

Plan and review alert routing, escalation policies, and security preferences.

Notification policy

Suggested priority: critical alerts to authorized administrators immediately, high severity to the security review queue, and routine events to scheduled summaries.

These are proposed settings—not live notification rules. Implement and verify the delivery integrations before enabling them.

Governance

Every impactful action requires traceable authorization, reason, timestamp, and verification of the result.

Alert severity and escalation design

Critical: Immediate notification to authorized security administrators; human assessment before containment.
High: Security review queue with administrator notification.
Unresolved: Escalate to responsible security leader according to approved deadlines.
Monitoring failure: Show stale-data warnings and investigate why events stopped arriving.

These are proposed notification rules, not configured alerts. Settings cannot be saved until an authenticated backend is connected.
