Community Groups & Meetups

Café y Coraje (Latin Pride Project) and QTBIPOC Kickbacks (BLK-QUEER COLLECTIVE) are MQA+ community groups. They also host shared activities where participants can connect, build friendships, and enjoy community together.

Register for shared activities with Café y Coraje and QTBIPOC Kickbacks. General admission is $10. Active My MQA+ members may qualify for complimentary admission, subject to the two-activities-per-calendar-month benefit.

Membership benefit verification: Zeffy checks membership eligibility for its members-only ticket, but the shared monthly limit of two complimentary activities is not yet enforced automatically. Please do not claim more than two complimentary meetup registrations in a calendar month. Additional registrations cost $10.

Registration and payment are processed by Zeffy. Your confirmation will come from Zeffy; automatic synchronization with My Registrations is still being developed.
