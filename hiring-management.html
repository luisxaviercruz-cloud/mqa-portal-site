MQA+ Leadership, Board & Access Structure

The Founder, Executive & CEO and Co-Founder & COO are the organization's two founder designations. The COO may also serve as Board Chair. One account may hold multiple positions.

PositionAccess category
Founder, Executive & CEOExecutive leadership and administration
Co-Founder & COOExecutive leadership and administration; may also serve as Board Chair
Board Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Board MemberBoard governance and authorized records
System AdministratorTechnical system administration, not executive authorization
Department Director and CoordinatorAssigned department only
Staff, Volunteer and AmbassadorAssigned responsibilities only

This is the approved role structure. Actual account assignments and executive authorization are enforced by the backend, not by this display.
